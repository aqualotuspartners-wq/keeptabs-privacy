KeepTabs Privacy Policy

Aqua Lotus Partners · Effective November 24, 2026

KeepTabs (“the app”) is published by Aqua Lotus Partners (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with information. The app is a personal tracker for subscriptions and recurring bills that you enter yourself.

Information that stays on your device

Subscription names, amounts, currencies, billing cycles, dates, categories, notes, colors, and reminder preferences are stored in a local database on your phone. We do not operate an account system, and we do not upload that list to Aqua Lotus servers. The app does not connect to your bank, and it does not read your email or text messages.

KeepTabs does not cancel, pause, or change a subscription with a provider. Reminders are notifications on your device so you can decide what to do yourself.

If you turn on Android backup or use device-to-device transfer, Android may include the local database and the app’s preference file in the backup controlled by your Google account or your device. You can turn backup off in system settings. Exporting a CSV uses the Android share sheet and sends the file only to the app you choose.

Advertising and consent

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob. Before ad requests, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether a consent message is required, including in the EEA and the UK. If a message is required, ads are not requested until you respond.

AdMob and UMP can collect and process device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, IP address (which may imply an approximate location), and information about how you interact with ads. That processing is done by Google under Google’s policies, not by an Aqua Lotus account. The app declares the Advertising ID permission for this purpose. We do not sell personal information.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Where the consent form offers an ad-privacy choice, you can reopen it from Settings in the app when that option is available.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders before a renewal or a trial end date. Those reminders are created on the device. We do not run a push-notification service for them.

Children

KeepTabs is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What this app is not

Totals in the app are calculations based on prices you type. They are not financial, legal, or tax advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
